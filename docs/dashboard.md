# Management dashboard

The dashboard now presents an exploratory financial overview requested by the owner, replacing the catalogue-count-only screen. Sales remains the initial screen; open Gestão to see Dashboard.

The Today, 7 days and 30 days controls switch independent, internally reconciled financial examples anchored to 17 September 2026. The introductory demonstration banner has been removed at the owner’s request; the existing footnote and contextual explanations still identify sample values. The introduction has no extra top margin, leaving the header’s 12px spacing. Completing prototype sales does not update the dashboard. No backend, persistence, stock decrement or transaction ledger was added.

The overview includes net sales and period comparison, estimated profit and margin, transaction count, average sale, cash balance, sales trend, gross profit, cost of goods sold, expenses, stock losses, discounts, returns, credit sales, debt collections, paid purchases, supplier debt payments and owner withdrawals. Cash movement reconciles opening balance plus receipts minus payments. Profit excludes tax, which is stated in the calculation explanation. A breakdown shows cash, M-Pesa and e-Mola balances. Outstanding customer/supplier balances and upcoming commitments are point-in-time examples, explicitly independent of the period selector. Product rankings are illustrative shares of revenue, not computed transaction rankings.

Stock counts and alerts derive from the current in-memory catalogue, refreshing when Dashboard opens. A provisional threshold of five selling units defines low stock. Product names and selling units remain visible. Alerts navigate to a filtered inventory; the inventory action opens management inventory. No purchase cost or stock valuation is invented from selling prices. Unavailable stock cost, expiry, slow sellers, stock coverage, recorded loss reasons and counted-cash discrepancy are shown as missing data rather than zero.

The four summary cards before the sales chart form a single column, as requested by the owner after reviewing the two-column layout. Detail panels stack on phones and use two columns on larger screens. Expandable native details provide calculations and exact chart values. Every text role is at least 16px; main text and controls use 18px. The chart uses lightweight CSS, without external dependencies.

Validation: served Chromium checks for all three periods at 320×568, 320×300, 1280×800 and 320px with 200% root text found no horizontal dialog overflow or visible text below 16px. Stock alert navigation and inventory navigation passed without runtime exceptions. The mobile screenshot was reviewed and introductory content shortened to prioritise the numbers. Actual Android device testing remains outstanding.
