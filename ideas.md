The system is intended to small and medium business but it is not limited to 
that, meaning even big businesses may use it when necessary. Another thing worth
mentioning is the fact that the system needs to work on a desktop and mobile environments.
The first versions must be optimized the most to have a good performance on the mobile
environment because of the small and medium businesses (mostly groceries, hence the name 
of the project) and the locality of course. Don't forget that we're crafting it make its
use in mozambique.


The features bellow they're not sorted by any criteria, I just write them up as the come 
to mind.


# receipt generator
The system should/must be able to connect to a receipt-POS-like
generator machine. The usecase I am thinking of right now is
for cases where the business has no change at the and gives the receipt
to the customer for change retrieval purposes. That's just what I thought
of right now, we have room to expand on that.

# payment automation
Automate the receipts payment in case the owner don't care receiving the money
from an electronic wallet. Which means, requesting the customer the wallet number
to receive the money.

We'll also need to able to have a list of trusted customers, we may decide to register
because the buy recurrently or maybe their of our trust, etc, it depends on the owner.

# Notes
Another feature we need to be able to provide is notes registration. Receivable and Payable notes.

# Inventory
Full inventory management.

# AI (I konw... cliche. But it's what's on the market write now)
An assistant helping with the evaluation, daily, weekly, month, etc. After a day of work the AI can
make a summary of what happend during the day, how much was made, how is the inventory, and many other 
metrics.

In a user predefined range of time the ai may present strategies to improve the business based what
has been happening during the last events.
